- Leclerc: 14 additional images from two public Sport Auto galleries, six delivery/street views and eight configuration views. Video stills retain that label and the source's photographer/video credits. A near-duplicate portrait from the configuration gallery was omitted. Existing Carscoops images were visually compared.
- Red/silver Monaco SP3: six views from ItalPassion, with the Verstappen attribution also reported by The Supercar Blog on 28 October. This is a **lead**, not a confirmed VIN record. Delivery date, model year, current location and headline valuation are not adopted as established facts. The repeated rear-view hero was omitted.
- Result: 190 records, 1,259 image references across 111 records. Original-production coverage remains **37/599**; no new VIN has met the evidence threshold in this batch.

## VIN research retained for follow-up

[Candidate queue](vin-candidates-2026-11-01.json) captures 47 complete VIN strings not present in the Atlas from one public Made in Maranello results page. These are not 47 confirmed cars. Individual chassis pages give model/year/VIN but no underlying document or plate image. Valid syntax or a check digit is not independent identity evidence. Obtain a dealer document, auction catalogue, public registration record or readable VIN plate before promotion.

Gohm SG0028 image 36 contains a small identification plate, but its 1,400-pixel public image does not support reliable transcription of all 17 characters. All 43 distinct public Gohm images were already in the Atlas; none was counted again. A higher-resolution primary dealer image remains a useful next step.

Leclerc's public X016 Autogespot search returned the same six already-indexed sightings. Gated galleries were not accessed. ItalPassion's April 2026 Leclerc illustration repeats the existing fuel-stop photograph and was excluded. No Leclerc VIN was found.
